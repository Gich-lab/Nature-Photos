How to play

  • Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box has the numbers 1 to 9, once each.
  • The bold numbers are given and can't be changed.
  • Tap a cell, then tap a number. Wrong numbers turn red.
  • Stuck? Use Hint. Made a slip? Use Undo or Erase.
  • Karibu kwenye huu mchezo na ujue hakuna kubahatisha! It's a logical deduction game.
